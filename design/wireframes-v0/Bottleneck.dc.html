4 · Options

A · Common interfaces

Recognition of other entities' processes becomes the default; one data standard. Each entity keeps its own operations.

Decided by: each governing body; executive heads

For: least disruption. Against: duplicated operators remain.

Evidence strength: to score

B · Certified shared providers

Services come from certified providers with published prices and service levels, used by default unless an exception is justified.

Decided by: governing bodies; system-wide management standards

For: scale, comparable prices. Against: provider capacity, transition cost.

Evidence strength: to score

C · One operating organisation

A single organisation provides enabling services to all participating entities.

Decided by: General Assembly, ECOSOC and participating governing bodies

For: largest scale. Against: highest transition cost, concentration risk.

Evidence strength: to score

Decision needed
Should system-wide operating rules apply by default to enabling services, with exceptions justified?
Who can take it: governing bodies of each entity; executive heads; major contributors through their board seats and agreements.
Evidence strength: partial. Key gap: coordination and reporting time is not measured.
