Level L-1 · Purpose and capabilities

What the system is for, and how each function is best delivered

Functions are grouped by the conditions they depend on. The grouping is analytical: it does not judge any entity's mandate, and most entities carry out functions in all three groups.

Distinctive roles

Functions that depend on universal membership, legitimacy or impartiality.

  • Norm-setting, treaties and international standards
  • Convening and mediation
  • Authorising and supporting collective action for peace and security
  • Protection and humanitarian access mandates
  • Global statistics and data standards
  • Presence where few others can operate
Delivery question: how are these protected and funded predictably?

Shared-delivery roles

Functions where governments, development banks, civil society and others also work.

  • Programme implementation
  • Technical assistance and policy advice
  • Service delivery at scale
  • Capacity development
Delivery question: where does the UN system add most value: leading, co-delivering, or supporting others?

Enabling services

Support functions that make the first two groups possible.

  • Procurement and supply chain
  • Finance and payments
  • Human resources administration
  • Information and communication technology
  • Logistics, premises, fleet and travel
Delivery question: own provision, shared provision within the system, or external sourcing?
