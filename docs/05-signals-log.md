# Signals Log: practitioner voices (LinkedIn and similar)

_Purpose: capture practitioner observations as **signals that generate or sharpen hypotheses**, then verify each against public, citable sources. Signals are never cited as evidence on their own._

## Rules
1. **Collected manually** by the author (no scraping; platform terms prohibit it).
2. **No names or identifying details.** Paraphrase the claim; do not copy text verbatim; do not store author names, profiles or links in this repo.
3. **Each signal maps to a hypothesis** (B1–B6, Q1–Q7) or opens a new one.
4. **Verify before use.** A signal enters the dashboard only through its verification sources. Status: `verified` · `partly verified` · `unverified`.
5. Self-selected and often advocacy-driven: treat frequency of a theme across many posts as a signal of salience, not of prevalence.
